Make customers investors — small checks drive outsized referrals

Source: Martin Tobias (@MartinGTobias)
original post

What it claims

Make your customers investors. Customers who invest in your company, even a very small amount, tend to drive outsized referrals. Do not focus so much on “strategic” large customer investments — take small checks from executives and mid-sized customers; if appropriate, run a crowdfunding campaign. Examples the author has invested in that crushed this strategy: LMNT (@drinkLMNT), LeaseUp (@LeaseUpCRE), and Equi.com.

Why it matters for a PO

B2B POs chase logo “strategic” investments and ignore the mid-sized champion who would put a small check in and then sell for you. Skin in the game turns users into distributors. Even without literal equity, the principle maps to paid pilots, design partners with fees, and champions with real budget — not free forever “friends.”

3 takeaways

  1. Customer-investors (even tiny checks) drive outsized referrals.
  2. Prefer many small checks from execs/mid-sized customers over only “strategic” large investments; crowdfunding can fit.
  3. Named pattern examples in the post: LMNT, LeaseUp, Equi — skin-in-the-game customers as growth engine.

Practice this week

List three mid-sized customers who love the product. Design one path for skin-in-the-game this quarter (paid pilot, advisory with a stake, or a small formal commitment). Stop treating “strategic logo, $0 commitment” as your only partner motion.
